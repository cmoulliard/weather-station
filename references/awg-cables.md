## Loose wires reference

| AWG Gauge | Conductor Diameter (mm) | Conductor Cross-Section ($\text{mm}^2$) | Max. Recommended Current (Signal / Chassis) | Max. Recommended Current (Continuous Power) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **22 AWG** | $\sim 0,64\text{ mm}$ | $0,33\text{ mm}^2$ | $5,0\text{ A}$ | $0,92\text{ A} - 2,0\text{ A}$ |
| **24 AWG** | $\sim 0,51\text{ mm}$ | $0,20\text{ mm}^2$ | $3,5\text{ A}$ | $0,58\text{ A} - 1,4\text{ A}$ |
| **26 AWG** | $\sim 0,40\text{ mm}$ | $0,13\text{ mm}^2$ | $2,2\text{ A}$ | $0,36\text{ A} - 0,9\text{ A}$ |
| **28 AWG** | $\sim 0,32\text{ mm}$ | $0,08\text{ mm}^2$ | $1,4\text{ A}$ | $0,22\text{ A} - 0,5\text{ A}$ |

**Note**: The recommended cable - loose wires for Dupont jumpers is: **24AWG** as it supports by example the 1.2A of the power-supply (220V -> 12V). This is also what it is used within the Makita DMR108 Radio !
