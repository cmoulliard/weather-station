### How it fits together
```
ESP32 ──I²C (SDA GPIO21, SCL GPIO22)──► TEA5767 board ──3.5 mm line out──► PAM8610 ──► 2 × 5 W speakers
  ▲                                         ▲ 5 V                             ▲ 12 V
  └──────────── 5 V ◄── step-down converter ◄──────── 12 V 3 A supply ────────┘
```
The ESP32 only **tunes** the radio over I²C. The audio goes straight from the TEA5767 jack to the amplifier.

### Points to watch
- **Why the PAM8610 rather than the PAM8406:** with common 4 Ω or 8 Ω speakers, a 5 V amplifier can't deliver 5 W per channel. You need 12 V.
- **I²C levels:** the TEA5767 board runs at 5 V. If its I²C pull-up resistors go to 5 V, use a level shifter (or remove the pull-ups) to protect the 3.3 V ESP32 pins. Check the board before wiring.
- **PAM8406 / PAM8610:** never connect the speaker "−" outputs together or to GND (both are bridged outputs).
- **The TEA5767 is an old chip.** The **RDA5807M** (also linked in your note) is a cheaper and more sensitive alternative that runs at 3.3 V, and it would avoid the level-shifting issue.
